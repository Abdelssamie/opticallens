Spectacle Lens Simulator

An educational tool for exploring how sphere, cylinder and axis shape the optical power of a spectacle lens — and what that power implies about an approximate 3D lens and frame form.

Approximate educational geometry — not a manufacturing model. Prescription values (SPH / CYL / AXIS / PD) describe optical power and do not uniquely determine the physical manufactured lens or frame geometry. The 3D model here is an educational approximation built from the parameters you supply.

3D geometry is an educational approximation based on the supplied optical and geometric parameters. It is not a prescription for manufacturing a real lens or frame. Real spectacle lenses and frames may involve atoric or freeform surfaces, prism, true decentered grinding, hinge mechanisms, thickness optimization and frame engineering that this simulator does not model. This application is an educational optical simulator, not a medical diagnostic or manufacturing tool.
